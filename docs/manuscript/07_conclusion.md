# Conclusion {#sec:conclusion}

The Crescent City Intelligence Platform is best understood as an
evidence-bound research artifact for navigating local public information. Its
central design choice is to preserve distinctions that are easy to erase:
empty is not unavailable, a source is not a summary, a summary is not a fact,
and a generated snapshot is not a live API.

The current implementation demonstrates a coherent path from municipal-code
acquisition and verification through source-health envelopes, alert analytics,
LLM-assisted presentation, and a parity-checked public export. Stable
fingerprints and atomic writes make unchanged reruns idempotent. The manuscript
and claim ledger make the same boundary explicit for scholarly readers.

The artifact does not establish safety impact, legal sufficiency, source
completeness, community representativeness, or LLM factuality. Those are
future empirical questions. Its present contribution is narrower and useful:
it gives local intelligence software a method for saying what it knows, what
it observed, what it could not check, how a generated sentence was produced,
and which next evidence would be needed to justify a stronger claim.
