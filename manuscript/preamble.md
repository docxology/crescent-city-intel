The shared renderer injects the following packages into the PDF:

```latex
\usepackage{amsmath}
\usepackage{amssymb}
\usepackage{amsthm}
\usepackage{booktabs}
\usepackage{longtable}
\usepackage{array}
\usepackage{graphicx}
\usepackage{listings}
\usepackage[capitalise,noabbrev]{cleveref}
\usepackage{natbib}
\newtheorem{theorem}{Theorem}
\newtheorem{remark}{Remark}
\newtheorem{example}{Example}
```
